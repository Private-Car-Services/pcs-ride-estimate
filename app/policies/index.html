Cancellation Policy

At Private Car Services, plans can change. Here's how cancellations work:

  • Free cancellation: You can cancel at no charge any time before your driver is within 1 mile of your pickup location.
  • Late cancellation fee: If you cancel when your driver is within 1 mile of your pickup, a fee of 25% of your estimated fare (minimum $10) is charged to the card on file.
  • No-show: If you are not at the pickup spot and we can't reach you after the driver has waited 10 minutes, the ride is treated as a late cancellation. (For airport pickups, see Airport Pickups.)
  • If we cancel: If your driver or Private Car Services cancels your ride, you are never charged.
  • Refunds: Any refund goes back to your original card and usually shows up within a few business days.
  • Think the fee was a mistake? Text (936) 261-7878 within 48 hours and we'll review it.

Questions about a charge? Text us at (936) 261-7878.

Refund Policy

  • Fares for completed rides are not refundable.
  • If something went wrong on your ride, text us at (936) 261-7878 within 48 hours. We review every report and will make it right when we can.
  • If we charged you by mistake (a billing error, a double charge, or a wrong amount), we will refund the difference.
  • Refunds go back to the original card. They usually show up within a few business days, depending on your bank.

Deposit Policy

  • Rides booked through our online quote page require a deposit of 25% of the estimated fare, charged when you book.
  • The deposit is applied toward your final fare. At drop-off, you pay only the balance.
  • If you cancel before your driver is within 1 mile of pickup, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • If you cancel after your driver is within 1 mile, or you are a no-show, the deposit is kept as the cancellation fee. If your deposit is less than $10, we keep only the deposit and nothing more is charged.
  • If the driver or PCS cancels, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • Rides booked in the PCS rider app do not require a deposit (see Payments, Tips and Pricing).

Your Private Information

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Pickup and drop-off addresses, flight details if you give them, and your ride history.
  • Your live location, but only during an active ride (and when you press the Alert button).
  • In-app chat messages between you and your driver.
  • Payment details are handled by Square, our payment processor. PCS never sees or stores your full card number.

How we use it

  • To book, run, and bill your rides, and to send you ride updates.
  • To keep riders and drivers safe, including sending your location to PCS if the Alert button is pressed.
  • To answer questions, handle problems, and keep required business and tax records.

Who we share it with

  • Your driver gets only what they need for the ride (such as your first name and pickup and drop-off spots).
  • Square, to process payments, and other service providers that help us run the app, only for that purpose.
  • Police or emergency services in an emergency, or when the law requires it.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never show your driver's phone number to you, or yours to the driver. Use in-app chat instead.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete your account and information. Text (936) 261-7878.
  • We may keep some records after deletion when the law requires it (for example, payment and tax records) for up to 7 years.

Rider Code of Conduct

We want every ride to be safe and comfortable for riders and drivers.

  • Be respectful. Treat your driver, other riders, and the vehicle with respect.
  • No harassment or discrimination. No threats, violence, sexual comments or contact, or hateful language, and no discrimination based on race, religion, national origin, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, or any other protected trait. This rule applies to drivers too.
  • Seat belts are required for everyone, every ride.
  • No smoking or vaping of any kind in the vehicle.
  • No illegal drugs.
  • Alcohol: No open containers of alcohol in the vehicle. Nobody under 21 may have alcohol.
  • Weapons: Weapons are allowed only as permitted by Texas law, and must stay holstered or secured and out of sight for the whole ride.
  • Passenger limit: The most passengers allowed is the number of seat belts in the vehicle. Every passenger, including children, needs their own seat belt.
  • Luggage: Bags must fit safely in the trunk or cargo area without blocking the driver's view.
  • Unsafe behavior: The driver may end the ride at a safe location if a rider's behavior is unsafe, illegal, or abusive. Fares for the distance traveled still apply, and riders may lose access to PCS.

Safety Policy

  • Emergencies: call 911 first. If you are in danger, call 911 right away. Then contact PCS.
  • Seat belts: Everyone must wear a seat belt on every ride.
  • Check your PIN: When a driver accepts your ride, the app shows you a rider PIN. Give that PIN to your driver to start the trip. Do not get in if the driver can't confirm the ride. This helps make sure you are in the right car with the right driver.
  • Alert button: Riders and drivers have an Alert button in the app. Pressing it opens your phone's dialer to call 911 and sends a red alert with your live location to PCS.
  • Driver safety check: If the vehicle stops for 4.5 minutes during a ride, the app asks the driver, "Are you OK?"
  • Private phone numbers: Drivers' phone numbers are never shown to riders. Use in-app chat to contact your driver.
  • Driver screening: Every PCS driver must pass a background check and hold a valid Texas driver's license and auto insurance.
  • Report problems: If anything felt unsafe, text (936) 261-7878 as soon as you can.

Children and Car Seats

  • Texas law requires children younger than 8 to ride in a child safety seat or booster seat, unless they are taller than 4 feet 9 inches. PCS follows this rule on every ride.
  • Riders must bring their own car seat or booster seat and install it themselves, unless a seat is arranged with PCS ahead of time.
  • The driver may refuse the ride if a child who needs a car seat does not have one.
  • Every child needs their own seat belt and counts toward the passenger limit.
  • Riders under 18 must ride with an adult.

Accessibility and Service Animals

  • Service animals are always welcome, as required by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Texas law. There is no extra fee. Drivers may ask only (1) whether the animal is needed because of a disability and (2) what task it has been trained to do.
  • Pets: Pets are allowed by request, if arranged ahead of time, and must stay in a carrier for the whole ride.
  • Wheelchairs and mobility devices: We can carry folding wheelchairs, walkers, and similar devices that fit safely in the vehicle. Our vehicles are not wheelchair-accessible vans, so tell us about your needs when you book and we'll do our best to help.
  • Need another kind of help? Text (936) 261-7878 before your ride.

Payments, Tips and Pricing

How fares work

  • Your fare estimate is based on mileage.
  • Distance is always rounded up to the next whole mile.
  • Texas sales tax of 8.25% is added.
  • Your final fare is recalculated from the actual trip at drop-off, so it may differ from the estimate if the route, distance, or stops change.

Stops and wait time

  • During a ride, if the driver sits still for 5 minutes, the app asks whether this is an additional stop.
  • If yes, a stop is added and wait time is billed at $0.40 per minute.

Paying in the PCS rider app

  • Your card is saved when you book. Nothing is charged at booking.
  • After drop-off, your card is charged the final fare plus tax plus an optional tip.
  • Tip choices: none, 15%, 20%, 25%, or a custom amount.

Paying through the online quote page

  • A deposit of 25% of the estimate is charged when you book and applied toward your final fare (see Deposit Policy).

Tips

  • Tips are always optional. 100% of your tip goes to your driver.

Airport fees and tolls

  • Airport fees and road tolls on your trip are passed through to you at cost and shown on your receipt.

Card security

  • Payments are processed by Square. PCS never stores your full card number.

Questions about a charge

  • If a charge looks wrong, please text (936) 261-7878 first. Most problems can be fixed quickly.
  • If you file a chargeback (dispute) with your bank without contacting us first, we will send the bank our ride records, such as GPS route, times, and receipts. Riders with unresolved chargebacks may lose access to PCS.

Airport Pickups

  • We serve IAH (George Bush Intercontinental) and Hobby (HOU) airports.
  • When you book an airport pickup, give us your airline and flight number. We track your flight and adjust your pickup time for early or late arrivals.
  • Free wait time: Your driver waits free for 30 minutes after your flight lands for domestic flights, and 60 minutes for international flights.
  • International arrivals: A $15 service fee (plus tax) is added to international arrival pickups, purely for extended wait times and extended parking fees.
  • After the free wait time, wait time is billed at $0.40 per minute.
  • Text us when you have your bags and are ready, and we'll tell you where to meet your driver.
  • If we can't reach you after the free wait time plus 30 more minutes, the ride may be treated as a no-show (see Cancellation Policy).
  • We are not responsible for missed rides caused by flight delays or changes we were not told about, or wrong flight details.
  • Any airport fees or parking charges are passed through to you.
