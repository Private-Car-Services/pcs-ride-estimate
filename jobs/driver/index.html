Requirements

You

Applicants must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid Texas driver's license. A city limo or chauffeur license is also required; you may already hold one or obtain it before you're approved to drive. A professional appearance and a customer-first attitude are expected.

Your vehicle

You must provide a clean, well-maintained four-door luxury vehicle with leather interior that is 10 years old or newer (model year 2016 or newer). The vehicle must have a current registration sticker and proof of insurance. Proof of documentation will be required, such as your driver's license, limousine license and vehicle registration.

Before your first ride

You must carry commercial auto liability insurance at or above Texas minimums (30/60/25), with Private Taxi Services LLC listed as an Additional Insured on your policy.

About the Opportunity

Overview

A PCS private driver (chauffeur) provides personalized luxury transportation for individuals, families and executives. The role emphasizes safety, punctuality, professionalism and discretion.

Service areas

In the Houston area, PCS drivers handle airport transfers to and from IAH (Bush Intercontinental) and HOU (Hobby), along with corporate, event and private rides. In the Waco / Dallas area, drivers provide airport transfers to and from DFW and DAL (Love Field), as well as corporate, event and private rides.

Service standards clients expect

PCS clients expect to arrive safely and on time at appointments, airports, events and other destinations, by the best route for traffic, schedules and their preferences, with the PCS driver app's GPS navigation and built-in meter. They expect a vehicle that is clean inside and out, fueled and in good working condition, and help with luggage, doors and personal items. Trip and mileage records are logged in the PCS app. Clients expect professional communication, calm handling of any concerns, and complete discretion and confidentiality about their activities and locations, with PCS ride support available when needed. For airport pickups, the client's flight number is used to track arrivals, so the driver is there on time and meets the client at baggage claim (preferred) or curbside, at the client's discretion.

Required skills and qualifications

The ideal driver has excellent driving skills and strong knowledge of local routes and traffic patterns in their area. You present a professional appearance and demeanor, bring customer service skills grounded in courtesy and discretion and have basic vehicle care knowledge.

Experience

Prior driving or chauffeur experience is preferred, along with familiarity with luxury or executive vehicle standards.

Your schedule

As an independent contractor with PCS, you decide when you're available and which ride requests you accept. Drive as much or as little as you like, and you're free to work with other services. The more rides you choose to take, the more you can earn.

Independent contractor (1099) opportunity. You set your availability. Drivers sign an independent contractor agreement before being approved to drive.

Earnings

Drivers earn 70% of each ride to start, plus an annual incentive: each year on your anniversary, a satisfactory performance evaluation (client feedback, on-time service, cleanliness and professionalism) plus a 95% or higher ride acceptance rate earns a 1% increase, up to 80%. You keep 100% of your tips.

Summary

A PCS driver ensures safe, efficient and comfortable transportation while maintaining high standards of professionalism, vehicle care and client service.
